给爸爸妈妈的话

2026年10月3日婚礼致辞 · 早上10:40

译文 · Translation

今天，我想借这个机会，对爸爸妈妈说几句话。

爸爸，谢谢您含辛茹苦地把我们抚养长大。我从小身体比较弱，常常生病，让您和妈妈多操了许多心。

在我心里，爸爸就像超人一样。您总是那么拼命地工作。只要能为家里多赚一点钱，什么工作您都愿意尝试。

小时候，您有时会责骂我。长大以后，我明白那是因为您希望我好，希望我有能力走出自己的路，在人生中闯出一片天地。

爸爸，现在您年纪渐长，该好好享享清福了。我希望以后有更多机会陪伴您、照顾您。祝您身体健康，长命百岁。

我也想说几句话给妈妈。

妈妈将近一个月前，因为跌倒导致脑出血，离开了我们。

她深爱着每一个孩子，也一直盼望着能参加我和晓諵的婚礼。今天我终于结婚了。我相信妈妈在天上看见，一定会为我们感到欣慰。

爸爸和妈妈一直相亲相爱。你们让我明白，夫妻之间应该怎样彼此疼爱、互相照顾。我也会这样去爱护晓諵。

谢谢爸爸，谢谢妈妈。我爱你们。

Today, I would like to take this opportunity to say a few words to my father and mother.

Dad, thank you for working so hard to raise us. I was a weak child and often fell ill, which gave you and Mum a great deal of worry.

In my heart, Dad, you are like Superman. You always worked so hard. If it meant earning a little more for the family, you were willing to try any job.

When I was young, you sometimes scolded me. Now that I am grown, I understand it was because you wanted the best for me, and wanted me to be able to find my own way and make something of my life.

Dad, you are getting older now, and it is time for you to rest and enjoy life. I hope to have more chances to spend time with you and take care of you. I wish you good health and a long life.

I would also like to say a few words to Mum.

Nearly a month ago, Mum had a fall that caused bleeding in her brain, and she left us.

She loved every one of her children dearly, and she had always looked forward to being at Sieu Nang’s and my wedding. Today I am finally married. I believe Mum is watching from heaven, and she must be glad for us.

Dad and Mum always loved each other dearly. You showed me how a husband and wife should cherish and care for each other. I will love and look after Sieu Nang in the same way.

Thank you, Dad. Thank you, Mum. I love you.

愿神祝福大家。
